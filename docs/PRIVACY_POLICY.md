## 1. Data stored only on your device
The following is stored **locally on your device only**, in app-private storage, and is never transmitted to us or any third party:
- Farm, crop, and supervisor details
- Worker records, attendance, daily entries, wages and payments (including contract work)
- Fertilizer, spraying, irrigation, machinery, purchase, harvest, inventory and expense records
- Tasks and notes
- Photos you take or attach within the app
- Reports generated from the above

We do not operate a server that receives this data. If you uninstall the app or don't back it up, this data is lost — see Backup & Restore in-app.

## 3. Your controls
- AI photo analysis requires an explicit tap each time; you're told before the photo leaves the device.
- Weather/maps can be skipped; the app remains fully usable offline for farm/worker/financial management.
- You can export or delete all local data at any time via Settings → Backup & Restore.

## 4. Permissions and why
Camera (photos you take), Location (only when logging/editing a farm's GPS position — never in the background), Notifications (local reminders only).
